Open thimble's workbench for the person.

1. If you can open canvases, open the `thimble` canvas (with `tab` set when the person named one: answer, cards, citations, notes, threads, claims, coverage, labels, settings).
2. Also call `thimble_open` (same `tab`) and give the person its address in one line, so they can open it in a browser too. The address holds a secret token: show it to the person only, and never write it into files.

Write nothing else.
